PSA Swaps Cards

One customer's experience submitting a card to PSA for grading.

This website is owned and operated by a US citizen who submitted a ~$1,000 English Cloud Kingdom Hearts card in pristine condition to PSA. PSA swapped my card with a less than $100 Japanese version of the card in significantly inferior condition. This site exists to warn other customers about PSA's practices.

When I first noticed this swap on PSA's site, I immediately notified PSA with an error report. PSA ignored my error report, and I reached out after a month with no contact while my card moved to grading according to their site. I initiated an investigation. The investigation concluded that I sent a Japanese card. I did not; I could not have submitted a card I have never owned. But PSA concluded from their investigation — incorrectly and dishonestly — that I sent a Japanese card. I have never owned a Japanese Kingdom Hearts card. I only ever owned the English Cloud card, which my brother gave me about 20 years ago.

When a formal dispute was filed with PSA's parent company, PSA provided inadequate evidence and refused to provide the evidence that would actually prove their claim: video recording of the card I submitted being removed from my untampered box. Their refusal to provide this evidence is extremely telling: it is in fact physically impossible for them to provide this evidence, because the card they swapped my card with is, obviously, not the card I submitted. It is not a card I have ever owned.

This site will be updated with more details as I continue to pursue justice for the swapping (i.e., theft) of my $1,000 card by PSA.

When I posted a report of this theft/impropriety to Reddit after first reporting the error to PSA, my post was taken down and a moderator told me not to post misinformation. I didn't. They, however, acted childishly and irresponsibly in removing my post simply because they disliked the facts I reported, which I was sharing in search of help and guidance.

Everything on this page is 100% truthful, including this: when I last spoke to PSA on the phone (calling them because they would not reach back out to me after the initial investigation), they told me, “Yep, we did our investigation (we were gonna let you know, you needed to wait longer) and you sent a Japanese card.” I said, “I can't believe I didn't take photographs.” The PSA rep on the phone literally laughed at me and said, more or less, “Yep, you need to do that.” In other words, even PSA admitted over the phone that they are not trustworthy enough to send things to without thoroughly documenting what you're sending in to protect yourself. I didn't know this. This experience with PSA swapping (stealing) my card has been a tough lesson, and I hope sharing it keeps others from being betrayed by PSA as well.

TAKE PHOTOS! And I'd strongly recommend not using PSA if trusting that your card will be properly handled is important to you.

Do I have some weird incentive to make up this bizarre lie and spend money on this website? Or does PSA have a massive incentive to conceal any acts of impropriety, like this swapping of my card — hence the concealing and withholding of evidence?

Tough call… 😑
